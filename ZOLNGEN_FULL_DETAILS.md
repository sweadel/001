### 2. File Architecture | الهيكل الإنشائي للملفات
- **index.html:** The high-end public storefront (The Hub).
- **products.html:** The institutional hardware catalog with advanced filtering.
- **maintenance.html:** The dedicated service and repair portal.
- **admin.html:** The "Pro" command center for executives and managers.
- **client-dashboard.html:** Private portal for institutional asset management.
- **main.js:** Global UI logic, AI Assistant, and Multilingual engine.
- **admin.js:** Financial analytics, order workflow, and audit systems.
- **database.js:** The core "Local Server" abstraction layer.
- **index.css:** The "Elite Design System" (Glassmorphism & Fluid Animations).
